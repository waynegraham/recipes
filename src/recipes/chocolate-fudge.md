servings: 4
time: 15M
ingredients:
  - 4 1/2 cups sugar
  - salt
  - 1/4 pound butter
  - 8 ounces evaporated milk
  - 12-ounce bag of semi-sweet chocolate chips
  - 1 pint marshmallow cream
  - 1 cup nuts (optional)
sourceURL: https://recipeland.com/recipe/v/appalachia-mountain-fudge-3646
---

  1. In large bowl put in the chocolate bars (broken bits) and the marshmellow cream.
  2. Grease a 9X13 inch cake pan with butter or margarine.
  3. In a fairly deep pot put in the sugar, salt, milk, and butter.
  4. Mix well put on medium heat stirring constantly until it comes to a full boil.
  5. Let boil for 13 minutes (exactly) remove from stove pour over chocolate bits, milk chocolate and marshmellow cream.
  6. Beat until smooth, add nuts if desired, and refrigerate for about 2 to 3 hours.
  7. Cut into pieces.

Keep refrigerated so it will stay moist and creamy. Can be frozen; will not turn white.
